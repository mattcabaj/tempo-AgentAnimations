Tempo Motion v0.19.0

The Tempo mark is animated, never redrawn. It lives as four metaball forms that melt and split through a shared engine and always resolve back to the logo. Motions are authored two ways: a grammar of nine channels (travel, orbit, spin, splay, tilt, spread, scale, pulse, fill) for gestures that are pure data, and a physics escape hatch for the ones that need real gravity, orbits, or collisions. Every motion then inherits the same rails: breathe and tremble, fit-to-canvas, the metaball goo, a two-tone or mono colour system across six brand hues, and render targets from 16 to 48px. The Sequence now maps to Tempo's real agent states — Reveal, Thinking, Tool use, Waiting, Complete — chaining each through a shared rest pose (intro, loop, outro) into one seamless run. Reveal is the one motion that begins from a single point rather than the logo.
